## Icons and logos

| Asset                               | What it is for                                                                                |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [System icons](./system_icons.md)   | Icons for actions, status and feedback inside an interface, published in `@equinor/eds-icons` |
| [Product icons](./product_icons.md) | Icons that identify an application or a business area                                         |
| [Logos](./logos.md)                 | The Equinor logo, the star symbol and favicons in internal applications                       |

## Illustration

Brand and technical illustrations should follow Equinor's visual identity guidelines to keep your design work consistent. The [Equinor Brand Center](https://communicationtoolbox.equinor.com/brandcenter/en/equinor/component/default/50420) (internal Equinor site) has the guidelines for creating illustrations that align with Equinor's visual identity and communication standards.

## Photography

Our photography style is modern and authentic, with a clear Nordic touch that reflects our brand character. Photography works best when you need to show people, environments or products in context. You can download approved Equinor photos from the [Media Bank](https://equinor.fotoware.cloud/fotoweb/) (internal Equinor site).
